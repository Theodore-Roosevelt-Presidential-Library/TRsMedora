# Site photos, 2026 (Matt Briney)

These are photos taken in Medora of the Historic Medora interpretive panels, NPS panels and plaques, and some buildings as they stand today. They are used as sources in `tools/build_features.py` (cited as "Historic Medora ... panel").

Many panels reproduce archive photographs, which belong to SHSND, TRMF and others. Treat those images as reference only, not for publication.
