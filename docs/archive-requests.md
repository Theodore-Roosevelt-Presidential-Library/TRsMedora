What could not be found online, and the exact citations to request. Compiled October 15, 2026.

## Montana Historical Society (photoarchives@mt.gov; mhslibrary@mt.gov)

Online already:
- Huffman's 1886 Medora series: six prints online (mtmemory 72166, 72389, 72403, 72459, 72460, 72461).
- Haynes's 1880 and 1881 Little Missouri sets: complete runs online (nodes 95290–95324, 91737–91738, 108829–108838).

To request:
1. **Huffman photo no. 981-393.** It is the one print missing from the online Medora run 981-391 to 981-395. Ask for its subject and a scan.
2. **Huffman vintage negatives 374–382 (Medora, 1886).** Ask which negative matches each print. Only 374 (981-209) and 381 (981-236) are matched now. Ask whether more unscanned negatives sit in accession PAc 1981-66.
3. **Frank Jay Haynes papers, MC-146, box-folder 22/1, stereograph negative register 1877–1891.** Ask for scans of the pages covering numbers 500–560, 660–680 and 1120–1140, the 1880–81 Pyramid Park and Little Missouri views.
4. **Haynes negatives not online:** 543–545, 550–551, 553–554 and 556 (1880); 1121–1123, 1129–1130 and 1133 (1881). Do they exist? Are there any 1883 Villard excursion negatives taken at Little Missouri?
5. **Samuel T. Hauser Papers, MC-37, box-folder 7/14**, "Marquis DeMores, 1884, 1886."

## Minnesota Historical Society, Northern Pacific Railway records (Gale Family Library)

**Nothing about Medora or Little Missouri is digitized.** The priority items:

1. **Valuation Engineer (finding aid 01032)**
   - Drawings of Railway Structures, ND Valuation Section 2, location 155/ov4, Drawer 20: **No. 88 "Medora: Station Plat, 1905"** and **No. 89 "Demores: Station Plat, 1905."**
   - Station Plat Maps 1917, location 138.E.12.5, box 4, No. 16 (Gladstone–Beach): the Medora sheet.
   - Track Profile Diagrams 1917, location 129.F.6.6F, box 5: Roll 44 "Milepost 148–151: Medora–Little Missouri"; Roll 45 "Milepost 160–163: De Mores."
2. **Engineering Department (finding aid 01016)**
   - Track profiles 1870–1892, location 134.L.18.7B, box 1: Volume 4, Missouri Division, 1879–1881.
   - Track profiles, location 138.F.2.2, box 2: Mandan to Glendive, 1893. Ask for the sheets covering mileposts 140–165.
   - Station Plats, location 138.I.3.6, box 10: "Medora, North Dakota, Yellowstone Division, 1917."
   - Track profiles, location 138.I.6.1, box 3: No. 1183-25 "Medora line change, 1947." This may explain why today's track differs from the 1883 plat.
   - Oversized items, location 138.E.2.4, box 19: No. 8-1 "Plan and elevation: Passenger and freight depot, Missouri Division," the standard depot plan of the era.
   - Location Maps, location 132.I.4.4: No. 301-10 "Central Dakota (Fargo to Little Missouri) ... wagon roads from Bismarck to Black Hills."
   - Subject files: No. 11415 "Medora: Line change, Curve 148A, 1947–1948" (box 334, location 134.K.8.9B).
   - Bridge Engineer negatives, location 129.E.5.1B, box 1: "No. 5. Yellowstone Division, Main line: Mandan to Glendive." Ask whether the Little Missouri bridge is included.
3. **President's and General Manager's records**
   - Letters received, 1883 General Manager (Haupt) letters on de Mores's ranch and packing plant: box 9 (location 137.H.3.8F) and box 10 (location 137.H.3.9B).
   - President's subject file No. 1799-2, "Medora, North Dakota: Stockyards (Historical data), 1914–1919" (box 668, location 137.E.19.8F).
   - President's subject file No. 1844-2, "Medora: Using bridge over Little Missouri for highway purposes, 1915" (box 693, location 137.F.2.3B).
4. **Secretary (finding aid 01029)**
   - File 108, de Mores agreements assigned to the NP Refrigerator Car Co.
   - Fire Losses file 313, "Oil Tank Car, Little Missouri Station, D.T., 1888."
   - Historical file 2703-1-2, "Account of the establishment of the packing plant at Medora," 1920–22.
5. **Land Department (finding aid 01020)**
   - Scrapbooks, Folder 1, "Medora, Dakota" (location 136.G.15.2F).
