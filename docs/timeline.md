# Timeline: Little Missouri and Medora, 1879–1888

This timeline drives the map's four dates. Source keys are explained in [sources.md](sources.md). **(verify)** marks a single-source or conflicting item.

## Before Roosevelt

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 10, 1879 | Army establishes the Bad Lands Cantonment on the west bank. About 50 men of the 6th Infantry build cedar-log buildings around a parade ground (TRC says spring 1879, **verify**). | HM52; HMdb 87804; TRC-CANT |
| 1880 | Northern Pacific reaches the Little Missouri; the bridge is under construction (Haynes photo). Depot and section house are built. Frank Moore & Co. finish the Pyramid Park Hotel (Nov 1880). Comba post office opens. | Haynes (MHS 91737); HMdb 87982; HM52; NPSHB |
| 1881 | Haynes photographs "Bad Land City, Little Missouri": the depot and the hotel. | MHS 108837 |
| Oct 1882 | Moore's hotel "will soon be enlarged and immensely improved." | Bismarck Tribune 1882-10-06 |
| March 1883 | Army abandons the Cantonment (March 4 per HMdb, March 24 per TRC, **verify**). | HMdb 87804; TRC-CANT |
| April 1, 1883 | The Marquis de Mores pitches a tent across the river and founds Medora. | HAG ch. IV; St. Paul Daily Globe 1884-02-28 |
| May 1883 | Gorringe's syndicate buys the Cantonment buildings and refits them as a resort. | HAG ch. II; TRC-CANT; LANG |
| Summer 1883 | General stores, saloons and the Malloy hotel go up at Little Missouri. The de Mores slaughter house, the Chateau and the company office and store go up on the other side. | Dickinson Press 1883-06-16, 07-21, 08-18; Ingersoll photos (SHSND 00042) |

## A. Roosevelt arrives (September 1883)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sept 6, 1883 | Little Missouri greets, with a bonfire and gunfire, a party of dignitaries headed west for the railroad's completion. | HAG ch. I |
| Sept 7–8, 1883 | T.R. steps off the westbound train at about 3 a.m. and sleeps in the loft of the Pyramid Park Hotel. See [arrival-train.md](arrival-train.md). | AUTO ch. IV [P]; HAG ch. I; TRC |
| Sept 22, 1883 | "New buildings of every description are going up." The slaughter house has tracks running into it. "The new depot will soon be completed." | Dickinson Press 1883-09-22 |
| Nov 5, 1883 | Little Missouri opens a school. | Dickinson Press, in HAG |

## B. Medora booms (summer 1884)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Feb 7, 1884 | *The Bad Lands Cow Boy* begins. Dances are held at the Hotel de Mores. | Cow Boy |
| Spring 1884 | Peter Book's brickyard opens; the first kiln is fired May 21. "Medora is destined to be a brick city." | Cow Boy 1884-04-03, 05-08, 05-22 |
| Apr–June 1884 | Roberts Hall (billiard hall and theatre, 24×75) is built on lot 7, block 17. | Cow Boy 1884-05-01, 06-26; HMdb 88016 |
| 1884 | NP trains begin stopping at Medora; the depot is there. Little Missouri's depot is later sold and moved to the HT Ranch. | HMdb 87986, 87982 |
| July–Aug 1884 | Black Hills freight house (240×42) built south of the track. | Cow Boy 1884-07-17; Bismarck Weekly Tribune 1884-08-08 |
| July–Nov 1884 | St. Mary's Church built (cornerstone Sept 20, blessed Nov 2). Von Hoffman House commissioned (Aug). Brick cottages "rapidly nearing completion" (Sept). | NRHP; Cow Boy 1884-09-25 |
| Oct 1884 | Medora–Deadwood stage line begins (to May 1885). | HMdb 88033 |
| Nov 1884 | Fitzgerald begins the Metropolitan Hotel. Fisher builds two stores on Third Ave. | HMdb 87499; Cow Boy 1884-11-06 |
| Dec 1884 | New slaughter and cold-storage houses under way (refrigerator building 40×130). 251 permanent residents. Stockmen's Association meets in Medora; T.R. chairman (Dec 19). | Cow Boy 1884-12-04; NRHP; TRC o280082 |

## C. At its height (summer 1885)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 1885 | Roberts & Co.'s saloon in Medora burns. | Dickinson Press 1885-01-17 |
| Feb 1885 | The Metropolitan opens. Paddock's livery (88×50) is on Third St. | Cow Boy 1885-02-12, 02-26 |
| March 1885 | Fisher's roller rink opens with a ball. | Dickinson Press 1885-03-14 |
| Apr–June 1885 | The new packing plant is finished and opens June 1 (100 head a day per HAG). Hotel de Mores is brick-veneered and reopens June 1 as the Northern Pacific Hotel. Jorgens builds a new drug store. | HAG; BROOKS57; HMdb 88037; Cow Boy 1885-04-16 |
| May 1885 | Fire chiefs are elected for both "the west side of the river" and "the east side." Little Missouri is still occupied. | Cow Boy 1885-05-14 |
| June 1885 | "Buildings painted uniformly and neatly arranged about the broad, circular plateau. The population is about 700." | St. Paul Daily Globe 1885-06-05 |
| Aug 4, 1885 | Joe Ferris's two-story store opens. | medora.com; HMdb 88062 |
| Nov 1885 | New school building. The *Cow Boy* now prints "Published in Medora." | Bismarck Weekly Tribune 1885-11-20; LOC |

## D. After the hard winter (spring 1887)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 1886 | Packing plant closes. | HMdb 87799; NRHP |
| Dec 1886 | De Mores family leaves the Chateau. Last *Cow Boy* issue on Chronicling America is Dec 23. | SHSND; LOC |
| Winter 1886–87 | Severe winter devastates Badlands herds. | NPS; HAG ch. XXVI |
| Jan 12, 1887 | Fire destroys the *Cow Boy* office. Joe Ferris's store has "a close call." | HMdb 88045; Eaton to Sewall 1887-02-26 |
| Early 1887 | Fire destroys part of the Cantonment. | TRC-CANT |
| March 1887 | Flood surrounds the slaughter houses and refrigerators. | Bismarck Weekly Tribune 1887-03-18 |
| May–Dec 1887 | Medora buildings are taken apart and shipped by flat car to Dickinson, including Paddock's, Malloy's and Willard's. Some are headed for Billings, Montana. | Dickinson Press 1887-06-04, 06-18, 12-03; Bismarck Weekly Tribune 1887-05-27, 06-24 |
| 1887 | T.R. begins selling his cattle interests. | NPS; HAG |
| Fall 1888 | Cantonment hospital moved to Dickinson. | TRC-CANT |

## Afterward (for later photos)

- 1897: Hotel de Mores burns (Sept 13).
- 1903: T.R.'s presidential visit (April 7).
- 1907: Packing plant burns (March 17); the chimney survives.
- 1919: Hagedorn's photo survey.
- 1923: Roberts Hall dismantled.
- 1925: Depot burns.
- 1963: Rough Riders Hotel taken down and rebuilt as a replica.
