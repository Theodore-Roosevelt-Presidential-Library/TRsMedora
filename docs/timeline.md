# Timeline: Little Missouri and Medora, 1879–1888

This timeline drives the model's time slices. Each entry gives its source. Items marked **(verify)** rest on a single or secondary source.

Source keys: **HAG** = Hermann Hagedorn, *Roosevelt in the Bad Lands* (1921), [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/rooseveltinbadla00hage). **AUTO** = *Theodore Roosevelt: An Autobiography* (1913). **TRC** = Theodore Roosevelt Center encyclopedia. **NRHP** = National Register nominations (see [sources.md](sources.md)).

## Before Roosevelt

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1879 (spring–Nov) | Army builds the Cantonment on the west bank: eleven log buildings around a parade ground, to guard Northern Pacific crews. About 50 men of the 6th Infantry. | TRC "Cantonment"; Prairie Public |
| c. 1880–82 | Railroad reaches the crossing. A saloon, store, boarding house and the Pyramid Park Hotel grow up around the station. | Prairie Public; HAG ch. I **(verify the dates)** |
| March 24, 1883 | Army abandons the Cantonment. | TRC |
| April 1, 1883 | The Marquis de Mores founds Medora on the east bank, opposite Little Missouri. | HAG ch. IV; NPS Historical Handbook |
| May 1883 | Gorringe's syndicate (Little Missouri Land and Stock Co.) buys the Cantonment buildings. | HAG ch. II; TRC ($1,500) |
| 1883 | Chateau de Mores built ($5,000, 26 rooms). First, smaller abattoir built. Company office and store standing by winter 1883–84. | SHSND; HAG; NRHP |

## Slice A: Roosevelt arrives (September 1883)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sept. 6, 1883 | Little Missouri greets, with a bonfire and gunfire, a party of dignitaries headed west to celebrate the railroad's completion. | HAG ch. I |
| Sept. 7–8, 1883 | T.R. steps off the train at about 3 a.m. and sleeps in the loft ("bull-pen") of the Pyramid Park Hotel. | AUTO ch. IV [P]; HAG ch. I; TRC |
| Sept. 1883 | HAG gives a near-complete inventory of Little Missouri, with relative positions (see [geography-and-overlay.md](geography-and-overlay.md)). Medora is "a few houses" under construction on the east-bank flat. | HAG ch. I |
| Nov. 5, 1883 | Little Missouri opens a school. | *Dickinson Press*, quoted in HAG |

## Slice B: The boom (summer 1884)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Feb. 1884 | *The Bad Lands Cow Boy* begins appearing. LOC catalogs the early issues as published at Little Missouri. | LOC sn84024777; NRHP 77001018 |
| 1884 | Metropolitan Hotel (later Rough Riders Hotel) built by George Fitzgerald. | SAH Archipedia; HAG plate caption |
| April 1884 | Peter Book of Mandan announces a brickyard and pottery works. | NRHP 77001018, citing the *Cow Boy* |
| July–Nov. 2, 1884 | St. Mary's Catholic Church built by Peter Book and blessed on November 2. | NRHP (St. Mary's) |

| Dec. 19, 1884 | Little Missouri River Stockmen's Association meets in Medora; T.R. elected chairman. | TRC o280082; HAG ch. XIII |

## Slice C: Medora at its height (summer 1885)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| June 1, 1885 | New, larger abattoir opens (100 head a day). | HAG |
| 1885 | Joe Ferris builds a two-story store (25 × 75 ft) on the east bank, moving from Little Missouri. Opening reported as Aug. 4, 1885. | HAG; HMdb marker 88062; medora.com **(opening date single source)** |
| 1885 | Clothing, drug and hardware stores, a barber shop, a school and Fisher's roller rink. Bill Williams moves to Medora; after a fire he moves into a two-story building the Marquis built for him, with a hall upstairs. | HAG (Medora chapter) |
| 1885 | *Mandan Pioneer*: a "well-built, substantial" Black Hills freight depot, several brick houses, a Catholic church and "a large hotel." | Quoted in HAG |

## Slice D: Decline (late 1886 to 1887)

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1886 | Packing plant closes. | NRHP 75001300; SHSND |
| Dec. 1886 | De Mores family leaves the Chateau (the Marquis returns briefly in 1887 and 1889). | SHSND |
| Winter 1886–87 | Severe winter devastates Badlands herds. | NPS; HAG ch. XXVI |

| 1887 | T.R. begins selling his cattle interests. | NPS; HAG |

## After (for context and later photos)

- **1895:** Jury hung in effigy in front of the Hotel de Mores (trc:3013559), so the hotel was standing then.
- **1903:** T.R.'s presidential visit (April 7). He greets people in the "town hall."
- **March 17, 1907:** The packing plant burns. The chimney survives.
- **1919:** Hagedorn's photo survey. Many 1880s buildings are still standing and some are labeled in his panorama.
