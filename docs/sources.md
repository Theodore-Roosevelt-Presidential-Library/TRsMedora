## The finds that change the project

1. **The 1883 plat of Medora** (PLAT1883). It shows:
   - section lines, the river, the railroad and the Cantonment buildings;
   - "Little Missouri" on the west bank;
   - the "Slaughter House," the Marquis's house, a coal mine and a vegetable garden with an irrigation ditch;
   - two street grids east of the river.

   It is now fitted to today's survey corners (see [geography-and-overlay.md](geography-and-overlay.md)). Only a 469×525 web image is online, so order a high-resolution scan from SHSND (archives@nd.gov).
2. **F. Jay Haynes's 1880–81 photographs of Little Missouri** (Montana Historical Society). They show:
   - "Bad Land City, Little Missouri," fall 1881, with the depot and the Pyramid Park Hotel ([mtmemory 108837](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/108837));
   - the Cantonment, 1880 ([91738](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/91738));
   - the bridge under construction, 1880 ([91737](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/91737)).

   Rights: in copyright, educational use permitted.
3. **L. A. Huffman's 1886 Medora series** (MHS, negatives 374–382). Five are online:
   - the depot ([72461](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72461));
   - Medora from the railway bridge ([72459](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72459));
   - the packing house ([72460](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72460));
   - the Chateau ([72389](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72389) and [72403](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72403)).

   Ask MHS about the rest of the series.
4. **SHSND collection 00042 (de Mores), mostly digitized on the [SHSND Photobook](https://statemuseum.nd.gov/photobook?CollectionNmbr=00042).** It includes Truman Ingersoll's 1883 views and the 1885–86 Vallombrosa album:
   - 00042-00151, View of Medora, 1883;
   - 00042-00071, bird's-eye view, 1885;
   - 00042-00195 and -00193/-00194, overviews from 1885 and 1886;
   - 00042-00076, Hotel de Mores, 1885;
   - 00042-00077, St. Mary's, c.1886;
   - Chateau views from 1883, 1884 and 1886.

   These overviews are the main evidence for placing the unlocated buildings.
5. **Army plans of the Cantonment.** These are 7 building plans in NARA RG 77, "Plans of Military Posts," file unit [4522386](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/4522386). They are digitized and unrestricted, and include the barracks, officers' quarters, hospital, storehouse and stables. HM52 gives every building's size. SHSND drew a map of the Cantonment reserve in 1879, and SHSND's 2025 drone and magnetometry survey of the site (PAC-MAC 2025) is unpublished, so ask for it.
6. **Newspapers are digitized.** The *Cow Boy*'s run on Chronicling America is 131 issues, Feb 1884 to Dec 1886, and its OCR can be bulk-downloaded from `tile.loc.gov`. It prints building sizes, streets, lots and fires. The *Dickinson Press* is the only digitized local paper for 1883, and its 1887 issues record Medora buildings shipped away by flat car.
7. **Hagedorn appears to have used pseudonyms.** Comparing his quotation of the *Dickinson Press* (Sept. 22, 1883) with the original:
   - where the paper has "Frank S. Moore rents the soldiers quarters," Hagedorn prints "Frank [Vine]";
   - where the paper has "P. Malloy will have a fine hotel," Hagedorn prints "[P. McGeeney]";
   - he says outright that "Bill Williams" is a made-up name. The real saloon keeper was probably Robert Roberts (Lang's "Big-Mouthed Bob"; Roberts & Co.'s saloon; Roberts Hall).

   Read HAG's people as stand-ins until checked.
8. **Photogrammetry already exists** for the Chateau and Chimney Park (Sketchfab user "ajkclark," 2023). It is not downloadable, so ask the owner.

## Added Oct 15 (late)

- **NPS "Tour of Medora," Aug 1957** (NMP-TR-7008, in NPSHB 1962 p. 54). Scaled map with north arrow. Marks the site of Little Missouri, the packing-plant foundation, ice house, holding pens and the town sites.
- **1956 Chimney Park site plan** (NMP-TR 3002-A, BROOKS57 Fig. 6).
- **"Medora about 1900"** annotated photo (BROOKS57 Fig. 8). Shows the packing plant, Rough Rider, Ferris store, Von Hoffman House, stage company, courthouse and twin NP water tanks.
- **Montana Historical Society:** six Huffman 1886 Medora prints online, including the new find "Marquis de Mores" ([72166](https://www.mtmemory.org/nodes/view/72166)). The full Haynes 1880 and 1881 Little Missouri runs are online.
- **W. R. Cross stereo, c.1880, "Cantonment, on Little Missouri River. N.P.R.R. Crossing"** (Black Hills State Univ., [digitized](http://explore.digitalsd.org/cdm/ref/collection/WRCross/id/47)).
- **Minnesota Historical Society NP records:** nothing on Medora is digitized. See [archive-requests.md](archive-requests.md).
- **Drone models** of the Chateau and Chimney Park (Sketchfab, 2023), cleared for Library use and shown on the map.

## Added Oct 15 (night): photo pass

- Every image reachable online is in `data/photo_catalog_1880s.csv`, with what it was used for. Harvard's server refuses requests from the workspace, so open those images in a browser.
